# Eco-Agent — 60-Second Pitch Script

**[OPEN on Streamlit dashboard showing SEVERE alert]**

> Everyone in this room knows what Skopje's air smells like in
> December. Pulse.eco tells you it's bad. Weather apps tell you
> it's cold. But nobody tells you **what to do about it.**

**[Switch to Report tab — file a report with evidence]**

> Eco-Agent changes that. When PM2.5 spikes, citizens anonymously
> report **exactly what's causing it** — illegal burning, factory
> emissions, waste dumping. They attach photos, links, detailed
> descriptions. Every report is timestamped with the live sensor
> reading. No login. No identity. Ever.

**[Switch to Evidence Dashboard — show reports, supports, discussion]**

> Every report goes to a **public evidence dashboard**. Other
> citizens hit "I've seen this too." They discuss anonymously —
> Citizen #1, Citizen #2. Evidence builds. Patterns emerge by
> neighbourhood, by category. This isn't a complaint box — it's
> a **collective evidence trail**.

**[Switch to Municipal Brief tab — generate and publish]**

> One click, and the AI takes live sensor data **plus** every
> citizen report **plus** attached evidence and writes a formal
> brief addressed to the Municipality of Skopje. Specific demands.
> Cited data. Publish it to the public board for everyone to see —
> then **email it anonymously** straight to the municipality. No
> one's name. Just the evidence.

**[Closing — show all five tabs]**

> Pulse.eco gives us the data. Eco-Agent turns that data into
> organised, anonymous civic pressure. Open-source AI, open data,
> zero identity stored anywhere.
> **The air won't fix itself — but the people who breathe it can.**

---

**Timing:** ~58 seconds at natural pace.
**Demo flow:** Alert → Report with evidence → Evidence Dashboard (supports + discussion) → Municipal Brief (publish + email) → Full tab view.
**Killer line for judges:** "The air won't fix itself — but the people who breathe it can."
**Second punch (if judges ask what's new):** "Every app shows you the data. We're the first one that turns it into an anonymous evidence trail the municipality can't ignore."
